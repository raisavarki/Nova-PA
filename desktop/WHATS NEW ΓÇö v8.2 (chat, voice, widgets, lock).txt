NOVA v8.2 — what changed
========================

You asked, this fixes it:

1. Chat tab now feels like a real AI chat
   • Centered conversation, rounded message bubbles, your messages on the
     right with a warm accent, Nova's on the left with her avatar.
   • Animated "typing" dots while she's thinking, a streaming caret while
     she writes, smooth auto-scroll (it won't yank you down while you read
     back), and a floating rounded composer.
   • A friendly welcome screen with four one-tap starters.

2. "What's on my screen" works even without an AI
   • If no AI is connected, Nova now reads the on-screen text with Windows'
     built-in OCR and shows it to you.
   • If a screen capture fails, she tells you instead of staying silent.
   • With a vision AI connected, she still sees and explains the screen.

3. The desktop helper no longer makes the mouse flicker
   • Click-through is now driven by a single owner in the background with a
     little hysteresis, and the "forward mouse" mode that caused the OS
     cursor to flicker between windows is gone. The avatar still catches
     clicks, drops and hovers exactly where you'd expect.

4. ElevenLabs voice (natural speech)
   • Settings → Voice → "ElevenLabs (natural)". Paste your API key, press
     "Load voices", pick one. Nova then reads chat replies and the desktop
     avatar's speech in that voice. Stays silent until you add a key.
     The key is stored encrypted on your PC and never leaves it except to
     ElevenLabs. Falls back to the free Windows voice automatically.

5. More personality
   • Every avatar (home, chat, lock, desktop) now blinks naturally and does
     the odd idle flourish — look-around, tilt, little nod — so Nova feels
     alive, not frozen. Respects "reduce motion".

6. Floating desktop widgets, polished + two new ones
   • Frosted, layered cards with softer shadows.
   • NEW: "Focus timer" card (start 25/45, live countdown, stop) and
     "Next meeting" card (how long till it, title, time, Join button).

7. A tidier sidebar
   • A core set is shown by default (Today, Calendar, Reminders, Tasks,
     Focus, Documents, Widgets, Chat, Settings). "More" reveals everything
     else, and it's remembered. Nothing was removed — just tucked away.

8. Lock on every open
   • Settings → "App lock (PIN)". Set a 4+ digit PIN and Nova asks for it
     every time you open the app (and every time you bring it back from the
     tray). Separate from the AI-key vault PIN.

Note: items 2, 3, 4, 6 and the lock-on-reopen involve Windows behaviour that
can only be fully exercised on a PC — please give them a quick test after you
build.
