Nova Companion — v7.6  (20 new avatar features)
===============================================

Nova's on-desktop avatar got a lot more alive. 20 new things:

NEW REACTIONS (17 animations)
  Nova now plays these on its own at the right moments, and you can
  trigger any of them yourself from the new Tricks menu (below):
   1. Laugh          — giggles, head rocking back
   2. Blush          — goes shy, cheeks glowing
   3. Spin           — a quick full twirl
   4. Backflip       — leaps up and flips over
   5. Shrug          — “who knows?” arms out
   6. Facepalm       — hand to face when something goes wrong
   7. Heart-eyes     — swoons, eyes turn to hearts
   8. Sneeze         — winds up… achoo!
   9. Shiver         — brrr, cold shake
  10. Juggle         — juggles three glowing orbs
  11. Meditate       — calm float with a zen aura
  12. Thumbs-up      — approval with a big green check
  13. Magic          — a sparkly “ta-da” flourish
  14. Peekaboo       — ducks down, then springs up
  15. High-five      — raises a hand for a slap
  16. Wink           — a quick cheeky wink
  17. Celebrate      — confetti + cheer when you hit a goal
                       (e.g. water goal reached, inbox cleared)

NEW SYSTEMS
  18. Colour skins   — dress Nova in Cream, Mint, Berry or Sky.
                       Your choice is saved. (Tricks menu → Colour)
  19. Time-of-day moods — Nova idles differently through the day:
                       morning stretches & yawns, midday curiosity,
                       evening winks & nods, sleepy at night.
  20. Purr when petted — stroke Nova’s head and it purrs, blushes,
                       and sometimes swoons with little hearts.

HOW TO PLAY THEM
  Right-click the avatar → the quick panel now has a **Tricks** tab
  with every reaction, plus the colour picker. Click any to play it.
  Nova also plays fitting reactions automatically as you use the app
  (e.g. dev mode → magic, rewards → celebrate).

Nothing was removed. Run it the same way:  npm install  then  npm start.
